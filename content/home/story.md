Somewhere between preparing for the JEE and leaving home, I started keeping things: photographs of ordinary evenings, notes I didn't want to lose, small programs written just to see how something worked.

Now my days go to code, Linux, AI and computer vision, and my evenings to a camera and a notebook. This site is where all of it ends up, in the order it happened.

**Still studying. Still writing. Still working out what comes next.**
